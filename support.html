Support

Next Budget iOS app  ·  Last updated: August 31, 2026

Need help with Next Budget? Email us and we will get back to you as soon as we can. You can write in English or Portuguese.

budgetnext@protonmail.com

What we can help with

  • Bug reports and unexpected behaviour
  • Questions about how the app works
  • Suggestions and feature requests
  • Sync, Sign in with Apple / Google, and restore purchases
  • Privacy or data-deletion requests

For a faster reply

Please include:

  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead
  • App version and build (Settings → About)
  • iOS version and device
  • Screenshots, if they help explain the issue

In the app

You can also open Settings → Help & Support to start an email with your device details already filled in.
