Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 4, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how João Miguel Monte ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use the Margin iOS application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Margin is designed with privacy in mind. We collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the service:

  • Financial data you enter — plans, activities, and cycles you create are stored locally on your device using Realm and, if you enable sync, in your personal Firebase Firestore database.
  • Account information — if you use the sync feature, a Firebase anonymous or authenticated account is created. No personal information is required to use the App without sync.
  • App usage analytics — we use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (e.g. screens visited, session length). This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Purchase status — whether you have purchased the full version is stored locally via StoreKit and UserDefaults. We do not store payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's features.
  • To sync your data across your devices (only if you enable sync).
  • To restore your purchase across reinstalls.
  • To understand how users interact with the App in aggregate.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is stored:

  • Locally on your device (Realm database).
  • In Firebase Firestore if you enable the sync feature. Firebase Firestore is a Google service governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Firestore, Analytics. Firebase Privacy.
  • Apple StoreKit — In-app purchases. Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Delete all your data by using the "Delete All Data" option in the App settings.
  • Disable sync, which stops any data from being sent to Firebase.
  • Contact us to request deletion of any server-side data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notice or by updating the effective date above.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at marginapp@protonmail.com.
